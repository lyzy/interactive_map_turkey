## Nemrut Dağ

**名称 Name**

English: Nemrut Dağ · 中文: 内姆鲁特山

**位置 Location**

Kahta, Adıyaman, Türkiye · 土耳其阿德亚曼卡赫塔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1987) · 是（1987）

**Intro**

Nemrut Dağ is a 2,150-metre mountain topped by the tomb-sanctuary of King Antiochus I of Commagene, built in the first century BC. Colossal seated statues of gods and kings, their heads now toppled from the bodies, flank a pyramidal tumulus, with reliefs of Persian and Greek deities. Sunrise and sunset from the summit are famously spectacular.

**简介**

内姆鲁特山海拔2150米，山顶是公元前1世纪科马基尼国王安条克一世的陵墓圣所。巨大的神祇与国王坐像分列金字塔形土丘两侧，头像现已与身体分离散落，周围还有波斯与希腊神祇浮雕。山顶的日出与日落景色极为壮观。

## Diyarbakır Fortress & Hevsel Gardens

**名称 Name**

English: Diyarbakır Fortress & Hevsel Gardens · 中文: 迪亚巴克尔城堡与赫夫塞尔花园

**位置 Location**

Diyarbakır, Türkiye · 土耳其迪亚巴克尔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2015) · 是（2015）

**Intro**

Diyarbakır's fortress is a vast circuit of black basalt walls, towers and gates dating largely to the Roman and Byzantine periods and later strengthened by Islamic rulers. Below the walls, the Hevsel Gardens form a fertile green belt along the Tigris, irrigated for centuries and still cultivated. The property was inscribed by UNESCO in 2015.

**简介**

迪亚巴克尔城堡由黑色玄武岩城墙、塔楼与城门组成，主体可追溯至罗马与拜占庭时期，后经伊斯兰统治者加固。城墙下方的赫夫塞尔花园沿底格里斯河形成肥沃绿带，数百年灌溉耕作延续至今。2015年列入世界遗产。

## Diyarbakır Ulu Cami & Sur

**名称 Name**

English: Diyarbakır Ulu Cami & Sur · 中文: 迪亚巴克尔大清真寺与苏尔区

**位置 Location**

Diyarbakır, Türkiye · 土耳其迪亚巴克尔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Ulu Cami of Diyarbakır is one of the oldest mosques in Anatolia, converted from a Byzantine church in the seventh century and rebuilt in the Seljuk period. Its courtyard, fountain and carved stone facades sit within the Sur district, a maze of black-basalt lanes, hans and churches inside the fortress walls. The area is being restored after conflict and earthquake damage.

**简介**

迪亚巴克尔大清真寺是安纳托利亚最古老的清真寺之一，7世纪由拜占庭教堂改建，塞尔柱时期重修。庭院、喷泉与石雕立面位于苏尔区——城堡墙内由黑色玄武岩街巷、客栈与教堂组成的迷宫。该地区在冲突与地震后仍在修复。

## Göbekli Tepe

**名称 Name**

English: Göbekli Tepe · 中文: 哥贝克力石阵

**位置 Location**

Şanlıurfa, Türkiye · 土耳其尚勒乌尔法

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2018) · 是（2018）

**Intro**

Göbekli Tepe is a Neolithic hilltop sanctuary built around 9500–8000 BC, older than Stonehenge and the pyramids. Circular enclosures contain massive T-shaped limestone pillars carved with animal reliefs, erected by hunter-gatherers before the invention of agriculture. The site transformed our understanding of the origins of religion and monumental architecture and was inscribed in 2018.

**简介**

哥贝克力石阵是约公元前9500至前8000年建造的新石器时代山顶圣所，比巨石阵与金字塔更为古老。圆形围栏内立有巨大的T形石灰岩柱，柱上雕刻动物浮雕，由尚未发明农业的狩猎采集者竖立。遗址改变了人们对宗教起源与纪念性建筑的认识，2018年列入世界遗产。

## Balıklıgöl

**名称 Name**

English: Balıklıgöl · 中文: 巴尔勒克勒湖

**位置 Location**

Şanlıurfa, Türkiye · 土耳其尚勒乌尔法

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Balıklıgöl is a pair of sacred carp pools in the centre of Şanlıurfa, associated with the prophet Abraham, who is said to have been thrown into a fire here and saved by God, the flames turning to water and the logs to fish. The surrounding park, Halilürrahman Mosque and cave of Abraham make it a major pilgrimage and family destination.

**简介**

巴尔勒克勒湖是尚勒乌尔法市中心的两座圣鱼池，传说先知亚伯拉罕被投入火中，蒙神拯救，火焰化为水、木柴化为鱼。周边公园、哈利勒·拉赫曼清真寺与亚伯拉罕洞穴使其成为重要朝圣地与家庭出游热点。

## Harran

**名称 Name**

English: Harran · 中文: 哈兰

**位置 Location**

Harran, Şanlıurfa, Türkiye · 土耳其尚勒乌尔法哈兰

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Harran is an ancient city of the Mesopotamian plain, mentioned in the Bible and famous for its beehive-shaped mud-brick houses that stay cool in summer. Its ruins include a university, mosque and city walls, and the site was a centre of astronomy and learning in the medieval period. The traditional village life nearby is part of the attraction.

**简介**

哈兰是美索不达米亚平原上的古城，《圣经》中曾有记载，以夏季凉爽的蜂巢形土坯房屋闻名。遗址包括大学、清真寺与城墙，中世纪时曾是天文与学术中心。附近的传统村落生活也是吸引力的一部分。

## Şanlıurfa Bazaar & Cuisine

**名称 Name**

English: Şanlıurfa Bazaar & Cuisine · 中文: 尚勒乌尔法巴扎与美食

**位置 Location**

Şanlıurfa, Türkiye · 土耳其尚勒乌尔法

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Şanlıurfa's covered bazaar is a lively network of lanes selling spices, textiles, copper and local sweets, with hans and tea houses tucked between the shops. The city is one of Türkiye's great food destinations, known for çiğ köfte, kebab, liver dishes and pistachio sweets. Eating here is as much a cultural experience as visiting the monuments.

**简介**

尚勒乌尔法的室内巴扎由热闹的街巷组成，出售香料、纺织品、铜器与地方甜点，店铺之间藏着客栈与茶馆。城市是土耳其重要美食目的地，以生肉丸、烤肉、肝类菜肴与开心果甜点闻名。在这里用餐与参观古迹同样是一种文化体验。

## Zeugma Mosaic Museum

**名称 Name**

English: Zeugma Mosaic Museum · 中文: 泽乌玛马赛克博物馆

**位置 Location**

Gaziantep, Türkiye · 土耳其加济安泰普

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Zeugma Mosaic Museum houses the spectacular mosaics rescued from the Roman city of Zeugma before it was flooded by a dam reservoir. Its galleries display the famous Gypsy Girl mosaic, ocean and mythological scenes, and reconstructions of Roman villas. It is one of the largest mosaic museums in the world and the highlight of Gaziantep.

**简介**

泽乌玛马赛克博物馆收藏了在罗马古城泽乌玛被水库淹没前抢救出的大量精美马赛克。展厅陈列著名的“吉普赛少女”马赛克、海洋与神话场景，以及罗马别墅复原。它是世界上最大的马赛克博物馆之一，也是加济安泰普的亮点。

## Gaziantep Castle & Bakırcılar

**名称 Name**

English: Gaziantep Castle & Bakırcılar · 中文: 加济安泰普城堡与铜匠市场

**位置 Location**

Gaziantep, Türkiye · 土耳其加济安泰普

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Gaziantep Castle occupies a hilltop mound with Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman layers, and its museum tells the story of the city's defense in the Turkish War of Independence. Below, the Bakırcılar bazaar is a working quarter of copper smiths, spice sellers and sweet shops. The city is also famous for its pistachio baklava and kebabs.

**简介**

加济安泰普城堡坐落于层叠着罗马、拜占庭与奥斯曼遗迹的山丘上，博物馆讲述城市在土耳其独立战争中的保卫战。城堡下方的铜匠市场是仍在运转的街区，聚集铜器匠人、香料商与甜品店。城市还以开心果果仁蜜饼与烤肉闻名。

## Mardin Old Town

**名称 Name**

English: Mardin Old Town · 中文: 马尔丁老城

**位置 Location**

Mardin, Türkiye · 土耳其马尔丁

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Mardin's old town climbs a hillside above the Mesopotamian plain in terraces of honey-coloured stone houses, mosques, churches and monasteries. Its lanes, hans and bazaar reflect a mix of Kurdish, Arab, Turkish and Syriac Christian heritage, and the view from the citadel at sunset is unforgettable. The city is one of the most atmospheric in Türkiye.

**简介**

马尔丁老城沿山坡层叠而上，蜜色石屋、清真寺、教堂与修道院俯瞰美索不达米亚平原。街巷、客栈与巴扎折射出库尔德、阿拉伯、土耳其与叙利亚基督教传统的交融，日落时从城堡眺望的景色令人难忘。这里是土耳其最具氛围的城市之一。

## Deyrulzafaran Monastery

**名称 Name**

English: Deyrulzafaran Monastery · 中文: 德伊鲁尔扎法兰修道院

**位置 Location**

Mardin, Türkiye · 土耳其马尔丁

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Deyrulzafaran is a working Syriac Orthodox monastery founded in the fifth century, set among olive groves east of Mardin. Its name means "monastery of saffron", and the complex includes churches, chapels, a cemetery and a sun temple beneath the main building. It remains the seat of the Syriac Orthodox Archbishop of Mardin.

**简介**

德伊鲁尔扎法兰是建于5世纪的叙利亚东正教修道院，位于马尔丁以东的橄榄园中，至今仍有修士生活。其名意为“番红花修道院”，建筑群包括教堂、小礼拜堂、墓地以及主楼下的太阳神庙。这里仍是马尔丁叙利亚东正教大主教的驻地。

## Midyat & Tur Abdin

**名称 Name**

English: Midyat & Tur Abdin · 中文: 米迪亚特与图尔阿布丁

**位置 Location**

Midyat, Mardin, Türkiye · 土耳其马尔丁米迪亚特

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Midyat is a stone town at the heart of Tur Abdin, the historic homeland of the Syriac Orthodox Church, famous for silver filigree and stone architecture. Its old quarter and surrounding villages contain ancient monasteries and churches, some still in use. The region offers a rare window into the living heritage of Syriac Christianity in Anatolia.

**简介**

米迪亚特是图尔阿布丁核心地带的石造小镇，这一地区是叙利亚东正教的历史家园，以银丝细工与石建筑闻名。老城与周边村落保存着古老修道院与教堂，部分仍在使用。该地区为了解安纳托利亚叙利亚基督教的活态遗产提供了难得窗口。

## Hasankeyf

**名称 Name**

English: Hasankeyf · 中文: 哈桑凯伊夫

**位置 Location**

Hasankeyf, Batman, Türkiye · 土耳其巴特曼哈桑凯伊夫

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Hasankeyf was an ancient town on the Tigris, with a cliff-top castle, cave dwellings and medieval bridges. Much of the lower town and its monuments were relocated or submerged after the Ilısu Dam reservoir filled in 2019–2020, and the new town sits above the water. The castle and some monuments remain, offering a sobering view of heritage and development.

**简介**

哈桑凯伊夫是底格里斯河畔的古城，拥有崖顶城堡、洞穴居所与中世纪桥梁。伊利苏大坝于2019至2020年蓄水后，下城大部分建筑被迁移或淹没，新城建在水面之上。城堡与部分古迹仍存，呈现遗产与开发之间的复杂现实。

## Kahta

**名称 Name**

English: Kahta · 中文: 卡赫塔

**位置 Location**

Kahta, Adıyaman, Türkiye · 土耳其阿德亚曼卡赫塔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kahta is the gateway town for Mount Nemrut, with a castle, a Roman bridge and the Karakuş tumulus nearby. Most visitors pass through on the way to the summit, but the surrounding countryside of the Kahta river valley rewards a slower stay. The town also has a museum and lively local markets.

**简介**

卡赫塔是前往内姆鲁特山的门户小镇，附近有城堡、罗马桥与卡拉库什土丘。多数游客只是途经前往山顶，但卡赫塔河谷的乡村风光值得放慢脚步。镇上还有博物馆与热闹的本地集市。
