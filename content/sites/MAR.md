## Historic Areas of Istanbul

**名称 Name**

English: Historic Areas of Istanbul · 中文: 伊斯坦布尔历史区

**位置 Location**

İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1985) · 是（1985）

**Intro**

The Historic Areas of Istanbul occupy the imperial peninsula between the Golden Horn, the Bosphorus and the Sea of Marmara. This UNESCO World Heritage property gathers the monuments of three imperial capitals — Byzantine Constantinople and Ottoman Istanbul above all — including Hagia Sophia, Topkapı Palace, the Blue Mosque, the Hippodrome and the Theodosian land walls. Its skyline of domes and minarets is one of the most recognisable in the world.

**简介**

伊斯坦布尔历史区位于金角湾、博斯普鲁斯海峡与马尔马拉海之间的帝国半岛，是联合国教科文组织世界遗产。这里汇聚了拜占庭君士坦丁堡与奥斯曼伊斯坦布尔两大帝都的精华，包括圣索菲亚、托普卡帕宫、蓝色清真寺、古竞技场和狄奥多西城墙。层叠的穹顶与宣礼塔构成了世界上最易辨识的天际线之一。

## Hagia Sophia

**名称 Name**

English: Hagia Sophia · 中文: 圣索菲亚

**位置 Location**

Sultanahmet, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔苏丹艾哈迈德

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Hagia Sophia was built by Emperor Justinian between 532 and 537 and remained the world's largest cathedral for nearly a thousand years. Its vast central dome, hovering mosaic seraphim and luminous stonework changed the history of architecture. After serving as a church, a mosque, a museum and again a mosque since 2020, it stands beside the Blue Mosque as the defining monument of Istanbul.

**简介**

圣索菲亚由查士丁尼皇帝于公元532至537年建造，在近千年间一直是世界上最大的教堂。巨大的中央穹顶、高悬的马赛克天使与流光溢彩的石材改变了建筑史。它先后作为教堂、清真寺、博物馆使用，2020年再次恢复为清真寺，与蓝色清真寺并列为伊斯坦布尔最具代表性的地标。

## Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed)

**名称 Name**

English: Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed) · 中文: 蓝色清真寺

**位置 Location**

Sultanahmet, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔苏丹艾哈迈德

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Blue Mosque was commissioned by Sultan Ahmed I and completed in 1617, facing Hagia Sophia across the Hippodrome. Its six minarets and cascade of domes are matched inside by more than twenty thousand hand-painted İznik tiles, whose blue tones give the mosque its popular name. It remains an active place of worship and one of the city's most visited monuments.

**简介**

蓝色清真寺由苏丹艾哈迈德一世下令修建，1617年落成，与圣索菲亚隔古竞技场相望。六座宣礼塔与层叠穹顶极为醒目，内部镶嵌两万余块手工绘制的伊兹尼克瓷砖，蓝色调使其得名“蓝色清真寺”。它至今仍是活跃的礼拜场所，也是伊斯坦布尔游客最多的古迹之一。

## Topkapı Palace

**名称 Name**

English: Topkapı Palace · 中文: 托普卡帕宫

**位置 Location**

Sarayburnu, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔萨拉伊布尔努

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Topkapı Palace was the residence of Ottoman sultans and the centre of imperial government from the 1460s until the 1850s. Its courtyards, harem, treasury and pavilions overlook the Bosphorus and the Golden Horn. The palace displays the imperial crown jewels, Ottoman robes, weapons and religious relics, and its tiled Baghdad and Circumcision pavilions are masterpieces of Ottoman architecture.

**简介**

托普卡帕宫自15世纪60年代至19世纪50年代一直是奥斯曼苏丹的居所与帝国政务中心。宫殿的庭院、后宫、宝库和亭阁俯瞰博斯普鲁斯海峡与金角湾，收藏着帝国珠宝、奥斯曼服饰、兵器与宗教圣物。巴格达亭与割礼亭的瓷砖装饰是奥斯曼建筑的杰作。

## Grand Bazaar

**名称 Name**

English: Grand Bazaar · 中文: 大巴扎

**位置 Location**

Beyazıt, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔贝亚泽特

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Grand Bazaar is one of the world's oldest and largest covered markets, founded after the Ottoman conquest of Constantinople and expanded over centuries. Its labyrinth of more than sixty streets and some four thousand shops sells carpets, ceramics, jewellery, leather and spices. The bazaar remains a working commercial quarter as much as a tourist attraction.

**简介**

大巴扎是世界上历史最悠久、规模最大的室内市场之一，始建于奥斯曼征服君士坦丁堡之后，历经数百年扩建。六十多条街巷与约四千家店铺纵横交错，出售地毯、陶瓷、珠宝、皮革与香料。它不仅是旅游景点，也是仍在运转的庞大商业街区。

## Basilica Cistern

**名称 Name**

English: Basilica Cistern · 中文: 地下水宫

**位置 Location**

Sultanahmet, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔苏丹艾哈迈德

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Basilica Cistern was built in the sixth century under Justinian to store water for the Great Palace. Its vast underground hall is carried by 336 marble columns, many reused from earlier Roman structures, and its dim, watery atmosphere is famous for the two Medusa-head column bases. Wooden walkways now let visitors explore the cistern.

**简介**

地下水宫建于公元6世纪查士丁尼时期，为大皇宫蓄水而建。巨大的地下厅堂由336根大理石柱支撑，许多柱子取自更早的罗马建筑；幽暗的水面与两根美杜莎头像柱础营造出神秘氛围。如今铺设了木栈道，游客可深入参观。

## Süleymaniye Mosque

**名称 Name**

English: Süleymaniye Mosque · 中文: 苏莱曼尼耶清真寺

**位置 Location**

Süleymaniye, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔苏莱曼尼耶

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Süleymaniye Mosque was built for Sultan Süleyman the Magnificent between 1550 and 1557 to a design by the architect Sinan, his acknowledged masterpiece. The mosque crowns the Third Hill above the Golden Horn, with a dome higher than that of Hagia Sophia and a great complex of madrasas, hospital, baths and tombs. Its courtyard offers one of the finest views of the historic peninsula.

**简介**

苏莱曼尼耶清真寺由苏丹苏莱曼大帝下令修建，1550至1557年由建筑师锡南设计，被视为其毕生杰作。清真寺雄踞金角湾上方的第三山丘，穹顶高度超过圣索菲亚，周围环绕着神学院、医院、浴室与陵墓组成的庞大建筑群。庭院是眺望历史半岛的最佳位置之一。

## Galata Tower & Beyoğlu

**名称 Name**

English: Galata Tower & Beyoğlu · 中文: 加拉塔塔与贝伊奥卢

**位置 Location**

Beyoğlu, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔贝伊奥卢

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Galata Tower was built by the Genoese in 1348 as part of their fortified colony on the north shore of the Golden Horn. Its conical roof and stone cylinder dominate the skyline, and the gallery gives a panoramic view of the historic peninsula. Below it, Beyoğlu's İstiklal Avenue is Istanbul's great promenade of cafés, bookshops, churches and nightlife.

**简介**

加拉塔塔由热那亚人于1348年建造，是其金角湾北岸殖民地的防御工事之一。圆锥形塔顶与石砌塔身主导着城市天际线，观景台可俯瞰历史半岛全景。塔下的贝伊奥卢区以独立大街为核心，是伊斯坦布尔著名的咖啡馆、书店、教堂与夜生活长廊。

## Dolmabahçe Palace

**名称 Name**

English: Dolmabahçe Palace · 中文: 多尔玛巴赫切宫

**位置 Location**

Beşiktaş, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔贝西克塔什

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Dolmabahçe Palace was built between 1843 and 1856 as the modern, European-style residence of the Ottoman sultans on the Bosphorus. Its ceremonial halls are famous for a 4.5-tonne crystal chandelier and a staircase of Baccarat crystal, while the palace also holds the room where Mustafa Kemal Atatürk died in 1938. The waterfront gardens and gates are among the most photographed in Istanbul.

**简介**

多尔玛巴赫切宫建于1843至1856年，是奥斯曼苏丹在博斯普鲁斯海峡畔的欧式新宫。礼仪大厅以4.5吨重的水晶吊灯和巴卡拉水晶楼梯闻名，1938年穆斯塔法·凯末尔·阿塔图尔克也在此逝世。临水花园与宫门是伊斯坦布尔最常被拍摄的景观之一。

## Bosphorus cruise

English: Bosphorus cruise · 中文: 博斯普鲁斯海峡游船

**位置 Location**

İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔

No · 否

**Intro**

A Bosphorus cruise follows the strait that divides Europe and Asia, passing Ottoman palaces, wooden yalı houses, fortresses and fishing villages. ## Balat & Fener

**名称 Name**

English: Balat & Fener · 中文: 巴拉特与费内尔

**位置 Location**

Fatih, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔法提赫

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Balat and Fener are neighbouring quarters on the Golden Horn whose Greek, Jewish and Armenian communities shaped Istanbul for centuries. Their steep streets are lined with brightly painted Ottoman houses, historic synagogues, Orthodox churches and the imposing Fener Greek Patriarchate. Cafés, antique shops and street art have made the area a favourite for slow walks and photography.

**简介**

巴拉特与费内尔是金角湾畔相邻的两个街区，希腊、犹太与亚美尼亚社群曾在此生活数百年，深刻塑造了伊斯坦布尔。陡峭街巷两侧是色彩鲜艳的奥斯曼老屋、历史悠久的犹太会堂、东正教堂以及庄严的费内尔希腊牧首座堂。咖啡馆、古董店与街头艺术使这里成为漫步与摄影的热门去处。

Kadıköy is the lively heart of Istanbul's Asian side, centred on a historic produce market and a web of streets filled with meyhanes, coffee roasters and bookshops. The Princes' Islands are a car-free archipelago in the Sea of Marmara, reached by ferry from the city. ## Chora Church (Kariye)

**名称 Name**

English: Chora Church (Kariye) · 中文: 柯拉教堂

**位置 Location**

Edirnekapı, İstanbul, Türkiye · 土耳其伊斯坦布尔埃迪尔内卡普

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Chora Church preserves the finest surviving cycle of Byzantine mosaics and frescoes in Istanbul, created in the early fourteenth century. Its scenes of the life of Christ and the Virgin, set in shimmering gold, are masterpieces of late Byzantine art. The building was converted into a mosque in 2020 and its museum function ended, so access can be restricted during restoration.

**简介**

柯拉教堂保存着伊斯坦布尔最精美的拜占庭马赛克与湿壁画组，创作于14世纪初。以金色为底的基督与圣母生平场景是晚期拜占庭艺术的杰作。该建筑于2020年改为清真寺，博物馆功能终止，修复期间开放可能受限。

## Archaeological Site of Troy

**名称 Name**

English: Archaeological Site of Troy · 中文: 特洛伊考古遗址

**位置 Location**

Tevfikiye, Çanakkale, Türkiye · 土耳其恰纳卡莱特夫菲基耶

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1998) · 是（1998）

**Intro**

Troy is a tell with nine superimposed cities, occupied from about 3000 BC to the Roman period, and the traditional setting of Homer's Iliad. Excavations have revealed the walls, gates and houses of the Bronze Age citadel, a Roman theatre and the great defensive ditch. A replica Trojan Horse near the entrance and the nearby museum complete the visit.

**简介**

特洛伊遗址由九层叠压的古城组成，从约公元前3000年延续至罗马时代，也是荷马《伊利亚特》故事的传统发生地。考古发掘揭示了青铜时代卫城的城墙、城门与住宅，以及罗马剧场和大型防御壕沟。入口处的特洛伊木马复制品与附近博物馆使参观更为完整。

## Gallipoli Peninsula

**名称 Name**

English: Gallipoli Peninsula · 中文: 加里波利半岛

**位置 Location**

Çanakkale, Türkiye · 土耳其恰纳卡莱

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Gallipoli Peninsula is the site of the 1915 campaign in which Allied forces tried to force the Dardanelles and were held by Ottoman defenders. Cemeteries, memorials and trenches commemorate soldiers from Turkey, Australia, New Zealand, Britain, France and other nations. ## Selimiye Mosque & Complex

**名称 Name**

English: Selimiye Mosque & Complex · 中文: 塞利米耶清真寺

**位置 Location**

Edirne, Türkiye · 土耳其埃迪尔内

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2011) · 是（2011）

**Intro**

The Selimiye Mosque was built by the architect Sinan for Sultan Selim II between 1569 and 1575 and is widely considered his finest work. Its single great dome, carried on eight massive piers, spans a space larger than that of Hagia Sophia, while the four slender minarets rise to 71 metres. The mosque, madrasas and bazaar form a complete Ottoman külliye inscribed by UNESCO in 2011.

**简介**

塞利米耶清真寺由建筑师锡南为苏丹塞利姆二世建造，1569至1575年完工，被公认为其巅峰之作。单一巨大穹顶由八根巨型墩柱支撑，覆盖空间超过圣索菲亚，四座细长宣礼塔高达71米。清真寺、神学院与巴扎组成完整的奥斯曼建筑群，2011年列入世界遗产。

## İznik

**名称 Name**

English: İznik · 中文: 伊兹尼克

**位置 Location**

İznik, Bursa, Türkiye · 土耳其布尔萨伊兹尼克

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

İznik is ancient Nicaea, famous as the site of the first and second ecumenical councils and for its well-preserved Byzantine walls and gates. The town's Ottoman monuments include the Green Mosque and the Nilüfer Hatun soup kitchen, while its tile workshops revived the celebrated blue-and-turquoise ceramics that decorate mosques across Türkiye.

伊兹尼克即古代尼西亚，以第一次和第二次大公会议召开地闻名，保存着完好的拜占庭城墙与城门。奥斯曼时期的绿色清真寺与尼吕费尔哈通济贫院也值得一看；当地瓷砖工坊复兴了著名的蓝绿釉陶瓷，土耳其各地清真寺的装饰常出自于此。

## Bursa historic core

**名称 Name**

English: Bursa historic core · 中文: 布尔萨历史核心区

**位置 Location**

Bursa, Türkiye · 土耳其布尔萨

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2014) · 是（2014）

**Intro**

Bursa was the first capital of the Ottoman Empire, and its historic core around Ulu Cami, the Green Mosque and the Yeşil Türbe contains the earliest masterpieces of Ottoman architecture. The UNESCO property, inscribed in 2014 together with Cumalıkızık, also includes hans, baths, madrasas and the tombs of the first sultans.

**简介**

布尔萨是奥斯曼帝国的第一座首都，以大清真寺、绿色清真寺和绿色陵墓为核心的历史城区保存着奥斯曼建筑最早期的杰作。该世界遗产于2014年与朱马勒克兹克村一同列入，还包括客栈、浴室、神学院及早期苏丹陵墓。

## Cumalıkızık

**名称 Name**

English: Cumalıkızık · 中文: 朱马勒克兹克

**位置 Location**

Bursa, Türkiye · 土耳其布尔萨

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2014) · 是（2014）

**Intro**

Cumalıkızık is a preserved Ottoman village at the foot of Uludağ, founded in the early Ottoman period and still inhabited. Its narrow cobbled lanes, half-timbered houses with whitewashed walls and stone courtyards, and small mosque give an unusually complete picture of rural Ottoman life. The village forms part of the Bursa and Cumalıkızık World Heritage property.

**简介**

朱马勒克兹克是乌卢达山脚保存完好的奥斯曼村庄，始建于奥斯曼早期，至今仍有人居住。狭窄的石板路、白墙木构架房屋、石砌庭院与小清真寺，完整呈现了奥斯曼乡村生活面貌。该村与布尔萨历史核心区共同构成世界遗产。

## Bursa Ulu Cami & Koza Han

**名称 Name**

English: Bursa Ulu Cami & Koza Han · 中文: 布尔萨大清真寺与丝绸市场

**位置 Location**

Bursa, Türkiye · 土耳其布尔萨

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Ulu Cami of Bursa was completed in 1399 and is one of the great early Ottoman mosques, famous for its twenty domes, central fountain and monumental calligraphy. Beside it, the Koza Han is a two-storey silk caravanserai built in 1491, still trading in silk scarves and textiles. Together they anchor Bursa's historic bazaar district.

**简介**

布尔萨大清真寺完成于1399年，是奥斯曼早期重要清真寺之一，以二十个穹顶、中央水池和巨幅书法装饰闻名。旁边的科扎汗是1491年建成的两层丝绸商队客栈，至今仍经营丝巾与纺织品。两者共同构成布尔萨历史巴扎区的核心。

## Uludağ

**名称 Name**

English: Uludağ · 中文: 乌卢达山

**位置 Location**

Bursa, Türkiye · 土耳其布尔萨

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Uludağ rises to 2,543 metres above Bursa and is the most accessible winter sports centre in western Türkiye. In summer its national park offers hiking, alpine meadows and views over the Marmara Sea, while a cable car connects the city to the slopes. The mountain was known in antiquity as Mysian Olympus.

**简介**

乌卢达山海拔2543米，是土耳其西部最易到达的冬季运动中心。夏季的国家公园适合徒步，可欣赏高山草甸与马尔马拉海景色，缆车将市区与山坡相连。此山在古代被称为密西亚奥林匹斯山。
