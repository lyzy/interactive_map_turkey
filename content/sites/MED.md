## Xanthos

**名称 Name**

English: Xanthos · 中文: 克桑托斯

**位置 Location**

Kınık, Antalya, Türkiye · 土耳其安塔利亚克讷克

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1988) · 是（1988）

**Intro**

Xanthos was the capital of ancient Lycia, set on a hill above the Xanthos river near the coast. Its Roman theatre, agora and pillar tombs — including the famous Harpy Tomb and the Nereid Monument, now in the British Museum — reflect a distinctive Lycian culture that blended Anatolian, Greek and Persian influences. The city was inscribed with Letoon in 1988.

**简介**

克桑托斯是古代吕基亚王国的首都，坐落于海岸附近克桑托斯河上的山丘。罗马剧场、市场与柱式陵墓——包括著名的哈耳庇厄墓和现藏大英博物馆的女神涅瑞伊得斯纪念碑——展现了融合安纳托利亚、希腊与波斯影响的独特吕基亚文化。1988年与莱顿共同列入世界遗产。

## Letoon

**名称 Name**

English: Letoon · 中文: 莱顿

**位置 Location**

Kumluova, Muğla, Türkiye · 土耳其穆拉库姆卢奥瓦

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1988) · 是（1988）

**Intro**

Letoon was the federal sanctuary of Lycia, dedicated to Leto and her children Artemis and Apollo, and the religious centre of the Lycian League. Three temples stand beside a sacred spring, and inscriptions found here were crucial to deciphering the Lycian language. It is the companion World Heritage site to Xanthos.

**简介**

莱顿是吕基亚联盟的联邦圣所，供奉勒托及其子女阿耳忒弥斯与阿波罗，也是吕基亚人的宗教中心。三座神庙围绕圣泉而立，此处发现的铭文对解读吕基亚文字至关重要。它与克桑托斯共同构成一处世界遗产。

**Intro**

Kaleiçi is the old quarter of Antalya, wrapped around a Roman-era harbour and enclosed by Byzantine and Seljuk walls. Antalya, Türkiye · 土耳其安塔利亚

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Antalya Museum holds one of Türkiye's outstanding archaeological collections, especially Roman sculpture from Perge and the surrounding region. **简介**

Myra was a Lycian city whose necropolis is carved into the cliffs above the plain, with house-shaped tombs and reliefs. Below lies the church of St Nicholas, built over the saint's tomb and later rebuilt by Byzantine emperors; its restored frescoes and sarcophagus attract pilgrims. The connection between St Nicholas and the modern Santa Claus began here.

**简介**

米拉是吕基亚城市，其墓地开凿在平原上方的崖壁，布满房屋形石墓与浮雕。山下是圣尼古拉教堂，建于圣人墓上，后由拜占庭皇帝重建；修复的壁画与石棺吸引众多朝圣者。圣尼古拉演变为现代圣诞老人的传说正源于此地。

## Kızkalesi & Cennet-Cehennem

**名称 Name**

English: Kızkalesi & Cennet-Cehennem · 中文: 少女城堡与天堂地狱洞

**位置 Location**

Erdemli, Mersin, Türkiye · 土耳其梅尔辛埃尔代姆利

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kızkalesi, the "Maiden's Castle", is a small Byzantine fortress built on a rocky island just offshore, linked to the legend of a princess protected from a prophecy. Inland, the Cennet and Cehennem sinkholes — "Heaven and Hell" — drop dramatically into the limestone, with a chapel inside the larger chasm and a winding path down.

**简介**

“少女城堡”是建在近海岩岛上的小型拜占庭堡垒，传说与一位被预言所困的公主有关。内陆的“天堂洞”与“地狱洞”是陡然塌陷的石灰岩天坑，较大的洞底有一座小教堂，并有蜿蜒步道下行。

## Tarsus

**名称 Name**

English: Tarsus · 中文: 塔尔苏斯

**位置 Location**

Mersin, Türkiye · 土耳其梅尔辛

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Tarsus is an ancient city of the Cilician plain, best known as the birthplace of St Paul and a meeting point of Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman history. Its landmarks include the St Paul's Well, the Roman road of Cleopatra's Gate, the Grand Mosque and the old bazaar. The nearby waterfalls and citrus groves make it an easy day trip from Mersin.

**简介**

塔尔苏斯是奇里乞亚平原上的古城，最著名的是圣保罗的出生地，也是罗马、拜占庭与奥斯曼历史的交汇点。圣保罗井、克利奥帕特拉门旁的罗马道路、大清真寺与老巴扎是主要看点。附近的瀑布与柑橘园使其成为梅尔辛周边轻松的一日游目的地。

## Antakya & St Pierre Church

**名称 Name**

English: Antakya & St Pierre Church · 中文: 安塔基亚与圣皮埃尔教堂

**位置 Location**

Antakya, Hatay, Türkiye · 土耳其哈塔伊安塔基亚

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Antakya is ancient Antioch, one of the great cities of the Roman East, where followers of Jesus were first called Christians. The grotto church of St Pierre, a pilgrimage site, preserves traces of early worship, while the city's bazaar and cuisine reflect a rich Arab, Turkish and Christian heritage. The area is recovering after the devastating February 2023 earthquakes.

**简介**

安塔基亚即古代安条克，是罗马东方的重要城市，耶稣的门徒最早在此被称为“基督徒”。圣皮埃尔洞穴教堂是朝圣地，保存着早期礼拜痕迹；城市巴扎与饮食折射出阿拉伯、土耳其与基督教传统的交融。2023年2月大地震后，这里仍在艰难重建。
