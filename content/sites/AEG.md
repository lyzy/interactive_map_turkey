## Hierapolis-Pamukkale

**名称 Name**

English: Hierapolis-Pamukkale · 中文: 希拉波利斯—棉花堡

**位置 Location**

Pamukkale, Denizli, Türkiye · 土耳其代尼兹利棉花堡

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1988) · 是（1988）

**Intro**

Pamukkale means "cotton castle", a dazzling hillside of white travertine terraces formed by calcium-rich hot springs. Above the terraces stand the ruins of Hierapolis, a Greco-Roman spa city with a huge theatre, Apollo temple, colonnaded streets and the Plutonium, a sacred cave linked to the underworld. The combined cultural and natural property has been UNESCO-listed since 1988.

**简介**

“棉花堡”意为“棉花城堡”，是富含钙质的温泉在山上沉积出的白色石灰华阶地，层层叠叠、耀眼夺目。阶地上方是希拉波利斯古城遗址，这座希腊—罗马温泉城拥有巨大剧场、阿波罗神庙、列柱大道，以及与冥界相连的圣洞“普鲁托尼翁”。该文化与自然双重遗产于1988年列入世界遗产。

## Ephesus

**名称 Name**

English: Ephesus · 中文: 以弗所

**位置 Location**

Selçuk, İzmir, Türkiye · 土耳其伊兹密尔塞尔丘克

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2015) · 是（2015）

**Intro**

Ephesus was one of the greatest cities of the Roman world and the capital of Asia Minor, with a harbour that has since silted up. Its marble streets lead past the Library of Celsus, the Great Theatre for 25,000 spectators, the Terrace Houses, the Temple of Hadrian and the Arcadian Way. The site also holds early Christian associations, including St Paul's ministry and the nearby House of the Virgin Mary.

**简介**

以弗所是罗马世界最伟大的城市之一、小亚细亚首府，其古港后来淤积成陆。大理石街道串联起塞尔苏斯图书馆、可容纳2.5万人的大剧场、露台住宅、哈德良神庙与阿卡迪亚大道。这里也与早期基督教密切相关，包括圣保罗的传教活动以及附近的圣母玛利亚故居。

## Pergamon

**名称 Name**

English: Pergamon · 中文: 佩加蒙

**位置 Location**

Bergama, İzmir, Türkiye · 土耳其伊兹密尔贝尔加马

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2014) · 是（2014）

**Intro**

Pergamon was the capital of the Attalid kingdom and a great centre of Hellenistic art and learning, famous for its library and the Altar of Zeus. Its acropolis climbs a steep hill above the plain, with the world's steepest ancient theatre, a vast trajan temple and an arsenal of royal palaces. Below lie the Asklepion healing sanctuary and the Red Basilica, a monumental Roman temple later converted into a church.

**简介**

佩加蒙是阿塔罗斯王国的首都，也是希腊化艺术与学术重镇，以图书馆和宙斯祭坛闻名。卫城依陡峭山势而上，拥有世界上最陡的古代剧场、宏大的图拉真神庙与王室宫殿群。山下是阿斯克勒庇俄斯疗养圣所与红色大教堂——一座后来改为教堂的罗马神庙。

## Aphrodisias

**名称 Name**

English: Aphrodisias · 中文: 阿弗罗狄西亚

**位置 Location**

Karacasu, Aydın, Türkiye · 土耳其艾登卡拉贾苏

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2017) · 是（2017）

**Intro**

Aphrodisias was a prosperous Roman city dedicated to Aphrodite, built beside a marble quarry that supplied its superb sculptors. Its monuments include a huge temple of Aphrodite, a 30,000-seat stadium, a theatre, baths and a monumental gateway, while the site museum displays one of the finest collections of Roman sculpture in Anatolia. UNESCO inscribed the city and its marble quarries in 2017.

**简介**

阿弗罗狄西亚是一座繁荣的罗马城市，供奉爱与美之神阿弗罗狄忒，毗邻大理石采石场，孕育了技艺高超的雕刻师。城中有宏伟的阿弗罗狄忒神庙、可容三万人的体育场、剧场、浴场与纪念性城门，博物馆则收藏着安纳托利亚最精美的罗马雕塑之一。2017年，古城与采石场一同列入世界遗产。

## Sardis & Bin Tepe

**名称 Name**

English: Sardis & Bin Tepe · 中文: 萨第斯与宾特佩

**位置 Location**

Salihli, Manisa, Türkiye · 土耳其马尼萨萨利赫利

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2025) · 是（2025）

**Intro**

Sardis was the capital of the Lydian kingdom, whose king Croesus became a byword for wealth, and later a major Roman city. Excavations have uncovered the monumental Temple of Artemis, a huge Roman bath-gymnasium complex, a synagogue and the gold-refining workshops of the Lydians. Nearby Bin Tepe holds the enormous burial mounds of Lydian kings, including the tumulus of Alyattes. The property was inscribed in 2025.

**简介**

萨第斯是吕底亚王国的首都，国王克罗伊斯以巨富闻名，后来发展为重要的罗马城市。考古发现了宏伟的阿耳忒弥斯神庙、大型罗马浴场—体育场建筑群、犹太会堂以及吕底亚人的炼金作坊。附近的宾特佩散布着吕底亚王陵巨冢，包括阿利亚特斯土丘。该遗产于2025年列入世界遗产。

## Basilica of St John & İsa Bey Mosque

**名称 Name**

English: Basilica of St John & İsa Bey Mosque · 中文: 圣约翰大教堂与伊萨贝伊清真寺

**位置 Location**

Selçuk, İzmir, Türkiye · 土耳其伊兹密尔塞尔丘克

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Basilica of St John was built by Emperor Justinian in the sixth century over the traditional tomb of John the Evangelist, on the hill of Ayasoluk above Ephesus. Its cruciform plan and massive brick piers still convey its former scale. Below stands the İsa Bey Mosque, completed in 1375, one of the finest examples of Anatolian Seljuk architecture with a richly carved portal.

**简介**

圣约翰大教堂由查士丁尼皇帝于6世纪建于以弗所上方的阿亚索卢克山，相传圣约翰福音书作者葬于此。其十字形平面与巨大砖墩仍可看出昔日规模。教堂下方的伊萨贝伊清真寺完成于1375年，是安纳托利亚塞尔柱建筑的代表作之一，正门雕刻尤为精美。

Şirince is a hillside village of whitewashed houses and red-tiled roofs above the valley of Selçuk, once a Greek settlement and now known for its fruit wines, olive oil and handicrafts. ## Priene–Miletus–Didyma

**名称 Name**

English: Priene–Miletus–Didyma · 中文: 普里埃内—米利都—迪迪马

**位置 Location**

Aydın, Türkiye · 土耳其艾登

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

This trio of Ionian sites lies south of Kuşadası. Priene is a beautifully planned Hellenistic city on a hillside, with a temple of Athena and a theatre; Miletus was a great trading city with a vast Roman theatre and baths; Didyma held the oracle of Apollo and the enormous Temple of Apollo, whose columns still stand. **简介**

这三处爱奥尼亚遗址位于库萨达斯以南。普里埃内是依山而建、规划严整的希腊化城市，有雅典娜神庙与剧场；米利都是重要贸易城邦，拥有宏大的罗马剧场与浴场；迪迪马曾是阿波罗神谕所，巨大的阿波罗神庙石柱至今仍矗立。三地共同勾勒出古代爱奥尼亚的思想与商业生活。

## Bodrum Castle & Mausoleum

**名称 Name**

English: Bodrum Castle & Mausoleum · 中文: 博德鲁姆城堡与摩索拉斯陵墓

**位置 Location**

Bodrum, Muğla, Türkiye · 土耳其穆拉博德鲁姆

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Bodrum Castle was built by the Knights of St John in the fifteenth century using stone from the Mausoleum of Halicarnassus, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. The castle now houses the Museum of Underwater Archaeology, with shipwrecks and artefacts from the Aegean. Only foundations and a few sculptures remain of the Mausoleum itself, once the tomb of King Mausolus.

**简介**

博德鲁姆城堡由圣约翰骑士团于15世纪建造，石料取自古代世界七大奇迹之一的摩索拉斯陵墓。城堡现为水下考古博物馆，陈列爱琴海沉船与出水文物。陵墓本身仅存基座与少量雕塑，这里曾是摩索拉斯国王的陵寝，“陵墓”一词即源于其名。

## Afyonkarahisar Ulu Cami

**位置 Location**

Yes (2023) · 是（2023）

**Intro**

The Ulu Cami of Afyonkarahisar is one of the five wooden hypostyle mosques inscribed by UNESCO in 2023. 阿菲永卡拉希萨尔大清真寺是2023年列入世界遗产的五座木柱清真寺之一。平直的木制天花由一排排雕花木柱支撑，体现了安纳托利亚中世纪融合塞尔柱与奥斯曼传统的建筑技艺。清真寺位于赋予城市名称的险峻岩石城堡下方。
