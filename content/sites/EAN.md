## Archaeological Site of Ani

**名称 Name**

English: Archaeological Site of Ani · 中文: 阿尼考古遗址

**位置 Location**

Ocaklı, Kars, Türkiye · 土耳其卡尔斯奥贾克利

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2016) · 是（2016）

**Intro**

Ani was the capital of the medieval Armenian kingdom of the Bagratids and a great Silk Road city, now a haunting ruin on a plateau above the Arpa river gorge. Its walls, cathedral, churches, caravanserai and mosque stand in a windswept landscape on the closed border with Armenia. UNESCO inscribed the site in 2016 for its outstanding medieval architecture.

**简介**

阿尼是中世纪亚美尼亚巴格拉图尼王国的首都与丝绸之路重镇，如今是阿尔帕河峡谷上方高原上的苍凉废墟。城墙、主教座堂、教堂、商队客栈与清真寺矗立在紧邻亚美尼亚边境的风蚀荒原上。2016年因其杰出的中世纪建筑列入世界遗产。

## Kars

**名称 Name**

English: Kars · 中文: 卡尔斯

**位置 Location**

Kars, Türkiye · 土耳其卡尔斯

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kars is a grid-plan city of Baltic-style stone buildings, shaped by its Russian imperial past in the nineteenth century. Its castle overlooks the town, and the surrounding highlands produce cheese and honey; the city is the gateway to Ani. Winter brings snow and a stark, atmospheric beauty to its wide streets.

**简介**

卡尔斯是呈网格布局的城市，19世纪俄国帝国时期留下的波罗的海风格石建筑塑造了它的面貌。城堡俯瞰全城，周边高地出产奶酪与蜂蜜；这里是前往阿尼的门户。冬季白雪覆盖，宽阔街道呈现苍凉而独特的美感。

## Erzurum

**名称 Name**

English: Erzurum · 中文: 埃尔祖鲁姆

**位置 Location**

Erzurum, Türkiye · 土耳其埃尔祖鲁姆

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Erzurum is a high plateau city at about 1,900 metres, with a Seljuk heritage of madrasas, mosques and a covered bazaar. Its Çifte Minareli Madrasa and Yakutiye Madrasa are among the finest Seljuk monuments in Anatolia, and the nearby Palandöken mountains make it a winter sports centre. The city is a gateway to eastern Anatolia.

**简介**

埃尔祖鲁姆是海拔约1900米的高原城市，拥有塞尔柱时期的神学院、清真寺与室内巴扎。双宣礼塔神学院与雅库蒂耶神学院是安纳托利亚最精美的塞尔柱建筑之一，附近的帕兰德肯山使其成为冬季运动中心。城市是东安纳托利亚的门户。

## Palandöken

**名称 Name**

English: Palandöken · 中文: 帕兰德肯

**位置 Location**

Erzurum, Türkiye · 土耳其埃尔祖鲁姆

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Palandöken is a ski resort on the mountain above Erzurum, with long runs, modern lifts and reliable snow from December to April. It hosted events during the 2011 Winter Universiade and is popular with Turkish skiers for its dry powder and high altitude. In summer the slopes become hiking and mountain-biking terrain.

**简介**

帕兰德肯是埃尔祖鲁姆上方山区的滑雪场，拥有长雪道、现代缆车，12月至次年4月雪况稳定。这里曾承办2011年冬季世界大学生运动会，以干燥粉雪与高海拔受到土耳其滑雪者欢迎。夏季山坡则成为徒步与山地自行车场地。

## Van Castle

**名称 Name**

English: Van Castle · 中文: 凡城城堡

**位置 Location**

Van, Türkiye · 土耳其凡城

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Van Castle is a vast Urartian fortress built on a limestone outcrop above the ruins of ancient Tushpa, with cuneiform inscriptions, rock-cut tombs and foundations of temples and palaces. The site overlooks Lake Van and the modern city, and the surrounding landscape includes Urartian rock monuments. It is one of the most important remains of the Urartian kingdom.

**简介**

凡城城堡是建在石灰岩高地上的巨大乌拉尔图堡垒，下方是古代图什帕遗址，存有楔形文字铭文、岩窟陵墓与神庙宫殿基址。城堡俯瞰凡湖与现代城市，周边还有乌拉尔图岩刻遗迹。这里是乌拉尔图王国最重要的遗存之一。

## Akdamar Church

**名称 Name**

English: Akdamar Church · 中文: 阿克达玛教堂

**位置 Location**

Gevaş, Van, Türkiye · 土耳其凡城盖瓦什

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Akdamar Church stands on a small island in Lake Van, built in the tenth century as the palace church of the Armenian kingdom of Vaspurakan. Its exterior is covered with carved reliefs of biblical scenes and animals, and the interior preserves fragments of frescoes. Boats run from the mainland to the island, which is a protected monument.

**简介**

阿克达玛教堂位于凡湖小岛上，建于10世纪，是亚美尼亚瓦斯普拉坎王国的宫廷教堂。外墙布满圣经故事与动物的浮雕，内部保存壁画残片。游船从大陆驶往这座受保护的古迹。

## Muradiye Waterfalls

**名称 Name**

English: Muradiye Waterfalls · 中文: 穆拉迪耶瀑布

**位置 Location**

Muradiye, Van, Türkiye · 土耳其凡城穆拉迪耶

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Muradiye Waterfalls are a series of cascades on the Bendimahi river northeast of Lake Van, set among orchards and poplar trees. The area is a popular picnic and tea spot, with simple restaurants and walking paths. It is often combined with a circuit of Lake Van and the surrounding mountains.

**简介**

穆拉迪耶瀑布是凡湖东北本迪马希河上的一系列跌水，四周果园与杨树环绕。这里是热门野餐与喝茶地点，有简易餐馆与步道。常与凡湖及周边山脉的环线游览组合。

## İshak Pasha Palace

**名称 Name**

English: İshak Pasha Palace · 中文: 伊沙克帕夏宫

**位置 Location**

Doğubayazıt, Ağrı, Türkiye · 土耳其阿勒多乌巴亚泽特

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

İshak Pasha Palace is a sprawling Ottoman palace built on a rocky terrace in the eighteenth century, with a mix of Seljuk, Ottoman, Persian and Georgian architectural elements. Its courtyards, harem, mosque and dungeon overlook the plain, with Mount Ararat rising behind. It is one of the most spectacular monuments of eastern Türkiye.

**简介**

伊沙克帕夏宫是18世纪建在岩石台地上的奥斯曼宫殿，融合塞尔柱、奥斯曼、波斯与格鲁吉亚建筑元素。庭院、后宫、清真寺与地牢俯瞰平原，亚拉拉特山在背后耸立。它是土耳其东部最壮观的古迹之一。

## Mount Ararat

**名称 Name**

English: Mount Ararat · 中文: 亚拉拉特山

**位置 Location**

Iğdır/Ağrı, Türkiye · 土耳其厄德尔/阿勒

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Mount Ararat is a snow-capped volcanic massif of 5,137 metres, the highest peak in Türkiye and traditionally associated with the landing of Noah's Ark. The mountain dominates the horizon of eastern Anatolia and is climbed by guided expeditions from Doğubayazıt and Iğdır, usually in summer. Permits and guides are required.

**简介**

亚拉拉特山是海拔5137米的雪顶火山群，为土耳其最高峰，传统上与诺亚方舟登陆地相关联。山体主导着东安纳托利亚的天际线，通常夏季从多乌巴亚泽特和厄德尔随向导攀登。登山需许可证与向导。

## Arslantepe Mound

**名称 Name**

English: Arslantepe Mound · 中文: 阿尔斯兰特佩土丘

**位置 Location**

Battalgazi, Malatya, Türkiye · 土耳其马拉蒂亚巴塔尔加齐

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2021) · 是（2021）

**Intro**

Arslantepe is a tell near Malatya with occupation from the Chalcolithic period to the Iron Age, and one of the earliest known palace complexes in the world. Excavations have yielded royal tombs, administrative archives and the oldest swords yet discovered, dating to around 3000 BC. UNESCO inscribed the mound in 2021.

**简介**

阿尔斯兰特佩是马拉蒂亚附近的考古土丘，从铜石并用时代延续至铁器时代，拥有世界上最早的宫殿建筑群之一。发掘出土了王室墓葬、行政档案以及迄今发现最早的剑，年代约公元前3000年。2021年列入世界遗产。

## Malatya

**名称 Name**

English: Malatya · 中文: 马拉蒂亚

**位置 Location**

Malatya, Türkiye · 土耳其马拉蒂亚

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Malatya is the apricot capital of Türkiye, its orchards spreading across the fertile plain below the Taurus mountains. The city has a long history reaching back to the Hittite era and preserves bazaars, mosques and a museum with finds from Arslantepe. It is recovering from the February 2023 earthquakes, which damaged much of the region.

**简介**

马拉蒂亚是土耳其的杏子之都，果园遍布托罗斯山下的肥沃平原。城市历史可追溯至赫梯时代，保存着巴扎、清真寺以及收藏阿尔斯兰特佩出土文物的博物馆。2023年2月大地震重创该地区，城市仍在恢复之中。

## Hazar Lake

**名称 Name**

English: Hazar Lake · 中文: 哈扎尔湖

**位置 Location**

Elazığ, Türkiye · 土耳其埃拉泽

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Hazar Lake is a mountain lake south of Elazığ, set in a valley of rocky hills and vineyards. Its shoreline is lined with cafés and picnic areas, and the lake is a local centre for swimming, boating and relaxed weekends. The surrounding region is known for its vineyards and traditional cuisine.

**简介**

哈扎尔湖位于埃拉泽以南的山谷中，四周岩石山丘与葡萄园环绕。湖岸遍布咖啡馆与野餐区，是当地人游泳、划船与休闲周末的中心。周边地区以葡萄园与传统饮食闻名。

## Kemaliye

**名称 Name**

English: Kemaliye · 中文: 凯马利耶

**位置 Location**

Kemaliye, Erzincan, Türkiye · 土耳其埃尔津詹凯马利耶

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kemaliye is a stone-built town in a deep gorge of the Euphrates, connected to the outside world by a dramatic road tunnel and narrow mountain roads. Its traditional houses, dark church and riverside setting attract travellers seeking a remote Anatolian town. Outdoor activities include rafting and canyon walks.

**简介**

凯马利耶是建在幼发拉底河深谷中的石造小镇，通过壮观的公路隧道与狭窄山路与外界相连。传统石屋、黑暗教堂与河畔环境吸引着寻找偏远安纳托利亚小镇的旅行者。户外活动包括漂流与峡谷徒步。
