## Göreme National Park & Rock Sites of Cappadocia

**名称 Name**

English: Göreme National Park & Rock Sites of Cappadocia · 中文: 格雷梅国家公园与卡帕多奇亚岩址

**位置 Location**

Nevşehir, Türkiye · 土耳其内夫谢希尔

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1985) · 是（1985）

**Intro**

Cappadocia is a volcanic landscape of fairy chimneys, tuff cones and deep valleys, sculpted by erosion and used by communities for millennia. The UNESCO mixed property includes Göreme National Park and the rock-cut churches, monasteries and underground settlements of the region. Sunrise balloon flights over the valleys have become one of Türkiye's signature travel experiences.

**简介**

卡帕多奇亚是由火山凝灰岩经侵蚀形成的奇特地貌，遍布“仙女烟囱”、锥状岩与深谷，人类在此开凿居所已数千年。该世界文化与自然双重遗产包括格雷梅国家公园及周边的岩窟教堂、修道院与地下聚落。清晨乘热气球掠过山谷，已成为土耳其最具代表性的旅行体验之一。

## Göreme Open-Air Museum

**名称 Name**

English: Göreme Open-Air Museum · 中文: 格雷梅露天博物馆

**位置 Location**

Göreme, Nevşehir, Türkiye · 土耳其内夫谢希尔格雷梅

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Göreme Open-Air Museum is a cluster of rock-cut monasteries and churches whose walls carry vivid ninth- to eleventh-century frescoes. The Dark Church, Apple Church and Buckle Church are among the finest, with scenes from the life of Christ painted in deep blues and reds. The site sits inside the Cappadocia World Heritage property and is its most visited monument.

**简介**

格雷梅露天博物馆是一片开凿于岩壁的修道院与教堂群，壁画多绘于9至11世纪，色彩鲜艳。黑暗教堂、苹果教堂与扣环教堂保存最佳，以深蓝与红色描绘基督生平。遗址位于卡帕多奇亚世界遗产范围内，是当地参观人数最多的古迹。

**Intro**

Uçhisar Castle is a natural rock citadel riddled with tunnels, rooms and windows, rising above the Cappadocian plateau as its highest point. ## Derinkuyu Underground City

**名称 Name**

English: Derinkuyu Underground City · 中文: 代林库尤地下城

**位置 Location**

Derinkuyu, Nevşehir, Türkiye · 土耳其内夫谢希尔代林库尤

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Derinkuyu is the deepest excavated underground city in Cappadocia, descending about 60 metres through multiple levels. It contained stables, churches, storage rooms, kitchens, wine presses and ventilation shafts, and could shelter thousands of people during raids. Narrow tunnels and rolling stone doors reveal the engineering skill of its builders.

**简介**

代林库尤是卡帕多奇亚已发掘最深的地下城，向下延伸约60米，分为多层。城内有马厩、教堂、储藏室、厨房、榨酒池与通风井，战乱时可容纳数千人避难。狭窄隧道与滚石门展现了建造者高超的工程智慧。

## Kaymaklı Underground City

**名称 Name**

English: Kaymaklı Underground City · 中文: 凯马克勒地下城

**位置 Location**

Kaymaklı, Nevşehir, Türkiye · 土耳其内夫谢希尔凯马克勒

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kaymaklı is one of the largest underground cities in Cappadocia, with eight levels open to visitors and a labyrinth of halls, stables, churches and storage rooms. It is less crowded than Derinkuyu and shows how entire communities could live below ground for long periods. The site is part of the wider Cappadocia World Heritage landscape.

**简介**

凯马克勒是卡帕多奇亚最大的地下城之一，目前开放八层，厅室、马厩、教堂与储藏间如迷宫般分布。这里比代林库尤清静，展示了整个社群如何长期在地下生活。遗址属于卡帕多奇亚世界遗产景观的一部分。

## Karatay Madrasa

**名称 Name**

English: Karatay Madrasa · 中文: 卡拉泰神学院

**位置 Location**

Konya, Türkiye · 土耳其科尼亚

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Karatay Madrasa was founded in 1251 by the Seljuk vizier Celaleddin Karatay and now houses a museum of Seljuk tiles and ceramics. Its dome is covered with some of the finest turquoise and cobalt tilework in Anatolia, and the collection includes star-shaped tiles, mihrabs and inscriptions. ## Neolithic Site of Çatalhöyük

**名称 Name**

English: Neolithic Site of Çatalhöyük · 中文: 加泰土丘

**位置 Location**

Çumra, Konya, Türkiye · 土耳其科尼亚丘姆拉

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2012) · 是（2012）

**Intro**

Çatalhöyük is one of the world's oldest known proto-urban settlements, occupied from about 7400 to 6000 BC. Its mud-brick houses were packed together with no streets, entered through roofs, and decorated with wall paintings, bull horns and figurines. The site has yielded key evidence for the origins of agriculture, craft specialisation and complex society.

**简介**

加泰土丘是世界上已知最古老的原始城市聚落之一，约公元前7400至前6000年有人居住。土坯房屋紧密相连、没有街道，从屋顶进入，内部装饰着壁画、牛角与雕像。遗址为农业起源、手工业分工与复杂社会的形成提供了关键证据。

**位置 Location**

Ankara, Türkiye · 土耳其安卡拉

No · 否

**Intro**

Housed in a restored Ottoman bedesten in Ankara's old quarter, the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations traces Anatolia from the Palaeolithic to the classical period. Its wide prayer hall is supported by more than sixty carved wooden columns, and the building preserves Seljuk-era stone and woodwork. The small town also has an attractive old bazaar and preserved Ottoman houses.

**简介**

西夫里希萨尔大清真寺是13世纪木柱清真寺，为2023年世界遗产系列申报的五座之一。宽阔的礼拜厅由六十多根雕花木柱支撑，建筑保存了塞尔柱时期的石作与木作。小镇还有颇具风情的古老巴扎与奥斯曼老屋。

## Great Mosque & Hospital of Divriği

**名称 Name**

English: Great Mosque & Hospital of Divriği · 中文: 迪夫里伊大清真寺与医院

**位置 Location**

Divriği, Sivas, Türkiye · 土耳其锡瓦斯迪夫里伊

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1985) · 是（1985）

**Intro**

The Great Mosque and Hospital of Divriği were built in the thirteenth century under the Mengüjek emirs and are celebrated for their extraordinary stone-carved portals. Their geometric, floral and figurative reliefs are among the masterpieces of Islamic stonework, and the complex includes a mosque, hospital, tomb and fountain. It was one of Türkiye's first UNESCO World Heritage sites, inscribed in 1985.

**简介**

迪夫里伊大清真寺与医院由门居杰克埃米尔于13世纪建造，以极其精美的石雕大门闻名。几何、花卉与人物浮雕是伊斯兰石作的杰作，建筑群包括清真寺、医院、陵墓与喷泉。它是土耳其最早列入世界遗产的项目之一，1985年入选。

## Sivas Historic Centre

**名称 Name**

English: Sivas Historic Centre · 中文: 锡瓦斯老城

**位置 Location**

Sivas, Türkiye · 土耳其锡瓦斯

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Sivas was an important Seljuk city on the Silk Road, and its historic centre preserves the Gök Medrese with its blue-tiled portal, the Ulu Cami, the Şifaiye Madrasa and the covered bazaar. The city is also known for its thermal springs and for hosting the Congress of Sivas in 1919, a milestone of the Turkish War of Independence.

**简介**

锡瓦斯是丝绸之路上的重要塞尔柱城市，老城保存着蓝色瓷砖大门的戈克神学院、大清真寺、希法耶神学院与室内巴扎。城市还以温泉闻名，1919年土耳其独立战争关键节点锡瓦斯大会也在此召开。

## Erciyes

**名称 Name**

English: Erciyes · 中文: 埃尔吉耶斯山

**位置 Location**

Kayseri, Türkiye · 土耳其开塞利

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Erciyes is a 3,917-metre dormant volcano rising above Kayseri, with ski slopes on its northern flank and alpine trails in summer. The mountain is visible from much of Cappadocia and has shaped the region's volcanic landscape. Its ski resort offers facilities for beginners and advanced skiers within easy reach of the city.

**简介**

埃尔吉耶斯山是海拔3917米的休眠火山，耸立于开塞利之上，北坡设有滑雪场，夏季则有高山步道。从卡帕多奇亚大片地区都能望见这座山，它也塑造了该地区的火山地貌。滑雪场距离市区不远，适合初学者与高级滑雪者。

## Kayseri Bazaar & Kültepe

**名称 Name**

English: Kayseri Bazaar & Kültepe · 中文: 开塞利巴扎与库尔泰佩

**位置 Location**

Kayseri, Türkiye · 土耳其开塞利

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Kayseri's historic bazaar is a vast covered quarter of hans, shops and food stalls beneath the Seljuk citadel, known for pastırma, sucuk and traditional crafts. Nearby Kültepe (Kanesh) was an important Assyrian trading colony in the early second millennium BC, where thousands of cuneiform tablets were found. Together they connect the city's Ottoman commerce with Anatolia's deepest history.

**简介**

开塞利老巴扎是塞尔柱城堡下方庞大的室内商业区，客栈、店铺与小吃摊林立，以帕斯特尔玛腌肉、苏朱克香肠与传统手工艺闻名。附近的库尔泰佩（卡尼什）是公元前两千年初重要的亚述贸易殖民地，出土了数千块楔形文字泥板。两者将城市的奥斯曼商业与安纳托利亚更深层的历史连接起来。

## Tuz Gölü (Lake Tuz)

**名称 Name**

English: Tuz Gölü (Lake Tuz) · 中文: 图兹湖

**位置 Location**

Aksaray/Ankara, Türkiye · 土耳其阿克萨赖/安卡拉

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Lake Tuz is one of the largest hypersaline lakes in the world, a shallow white expanse in the middle of the Anatolian plateau. In spring, rainfall and minerals turn parts of the lake pink, attracting photographers, and the salt flats are harvested commercially. The lake is an important habitat for flamingos and other waterbirds.

**简介**

图兹湖是世界上最大的超盐湖之一，位于安纳托利亚高原中部，是一片浅而洁白的盐滩。春季雨水与矿物质使部分湖面泛出粉红色，吸引众多摄影者，盐壳也被商业开采。湖泊是火烈鸟等水鸟的重要栖息地。
