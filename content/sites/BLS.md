## Hattusha: the Hittite Capital

**名称 Name**

English: Hattusha: the Hittite Capital · 中文: 哈图沙：赫梯首都

**位置 Location**

Boğazkale, Çorum, Türkiye · 土耳其乔鲁姆博阿兹卡莱

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1986) · 是（1986）

**Intro**

Hattusha was the capital of the Hittite Empire from the seventeenth to the twelfth centuries BC, a great Anatolian power that rivalled Egypt. Its massive walls, Lion Gate, King's Gate, Great Temple and the royal acropolis are spread across a rocky plateau, while the nearby rock sanctuary of Yazılıkaya preserves reliefs of gods and kings. The site was inscribed by UNESCO in 1986.

**简介**

哈图沙是公元前17至前12世纪赫梯帝国的首都，这个安纳托利亚强国曾与埃及争雄。巨大的城墙、狮门、王门、大神庙与王室卫城分布在岩石高原上，附近的亚兹勒卡亚岩壁圣所保存着神祇与国王浮雕。1986年列入世界遗产。

## Alacahöyük

**名称 Name**

English: Alacahöyük · 中文: 阿拉贾赫于克

**位置 Location**

Alaca, Çorum, Türkiye · 土耳其乔鲁姆阿拉贾

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Alacahöyük is an archaeological mound with occupation from the Chalcolithic period to the Hittite era. Its royal tombs contained gold, bronze and iron objects of remarkable craftsmanship, and the Hittite gate is flanked by carved sphinxes and a relief of a king and priest. The site lies close to Hattusha and is often visited together with it.

**简介**

阿拉贾赫于克是自铜石并用时代延续至赫梯时期的考古土丘。王室墓葬出土了工艺精湛的金、青铜与铁器，赫梯城门两侧立有狮身人面像与国王、祭司浮雕。遗址距哈图沙不远，常与哈图沙一同参观。

## City of Safranbolu

**名称 Name**

English: City of Safranbolu · 中文: 番红花城

**位置 Location**

Safranbolu, Karabük, Türkiye · 土耳其卡拉比克番红花城

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (1994) · 是（1994）

**Intro**

Safranbolu is a beautifully preserved Ottoman town of timber-framed houses, cobbled lanes and historic hans, set in a valley inland from the Black Sea. Its traditional houses, mosques, fountains and guild buildings reflect Ottoman urban life from the eighteenth to twentieth centuries. The town gave its name to saffron and was inscribed by UNESCO in 1994.

**简介**

番红花城是保存极佳的奥斯曼小镇，木构架房屋、石板街巷与历史客栈坐落于黑海内陆山谷。传统宅邸、清真寺、喷泉与行会建筑展现了18至20世纪的奥斯曼城市生活。小镇因番红花得名，1994年列入世界遗产。

## Kastamonu & Mahmut Bey Mosque

**名称 Name**

English: Kastamonu & Mahmut Bey Mosque · 中文: 卡斯塔莫努与马赫穆特贝伊清真寺

**位置 Location**

Kastamonu, Türkiye · 土耳其卡斯塔莫努

**UNESCO World Heritage**

Yes (2023) · 是（2023）

**Intro**

Kastamonu is an old Anatolian town of stone houses, mosques and a covered bazaar, and the nearby village of Kasaba holds the Mahmut Bey Mosque, built in 1366. The mosque is one of five wooden hypostyle mosques inscribed by UNESCO in 2023, celebrated for its carved columns and painted ceiling. The town itself preserves a rich Seljuk and Ottoman heritage.

**简介**

卡斯塔莫努是安纳托利亚古城，石屋、清真寺与室内巴扎保存完好；附近卡萨巴村的马赫穆特贝伊清真寺建于1366年，以雕花木柱与彩绘天花闻名，是2023年列入世界遗产的五座木柱清真寺之一。小镇本身也保存着丰富的塞尔柱与奥斯曼遗产。

## Sinop

Sinop occupies a rocky peninsula on the Black Sea, the northernmost point of Anatolia, with a Byzantine castle, ancient walls and a harbour sheltered by the headland. ## Amasya

Amasya, Türkiye · 土耳其阿马西亚

No · 否

**Intro**

Amasya is a river town famous for the rock-cut tombs of the Pontic kings carved into the cliffs above the Yeşilırmak, and for its Ottoman timber houses along the water. ## Samsun

**名称 Name**

English: Samsun · 中文: 萨姆松

**位置 Location**

Samsun, Türkiye · 土耳其萨姆松

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Samsun is the largest city on the Turkish Black Sea coast and the place where Mustafa Kemal Atatürk landed in 1919 to begin the War of Independence. Its Amisos Hill has archaeological remains and a panoramic view, and the city's museums, promenade and nearby delta wetlands attract visitors. It is a practical gateway to the central Black Sea region.

**简介**

萨姆松是土耳其黑海沿岸最大城市，1919年穆斯塔法·凯末尔·阿塔图尔克在此登陆，揭开独立战争序幕。阿米索斯山留有考古遗迹并可俯瞰全景，城市博物馆、海滨步道与附近三角洲湿地吸引游客。这里是前往黑海中部地区的实用门户。

## Trabzon Hagia Sophia

**名称 Name**

English: Trabzon Hagia Sophia · 中文: 特拉布宗圣索菲亚

**位置 Location**

Trabzon, Türkiye · 土耳其特拉布宗

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Trabzon Hagia Sophia is a thirteenth-century Byzantine church built by the Komnenos dynasty of the Empire of Trebizond, standing on a bluff above the Black Sea. Its dome, frescoes and stone reliefs show a distinctive Pontic style, and the building served as a mosque and museum before becoming a mosque again. The garden offers fine sea views.

## Sümela Monastery

**名称 Name**

English: Sümela Monastery · 中文: 苏梅拉修道院

**位置 Location**

Maçka, Trabzon, Türkiye · 土耳其特拉布宗马奇卡

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

Sümela Monastery clings to a sheer cliff in the Altındere valley, founded in the fourth century and expanded by the Komnenoi. Its painted courtyard church, rock-cut cells and aqueduct are reached by a steep path and stairs, with forested mountains all around. Restoration has affected access at times, but the setting remains one of the most dramatic in Türkiye.

**简介**

苏梅拉修道院紧贴阿尔滕德雷峡谷的陡崖而建，始建于4世纪，后由科穆宁王朝扩建。彩绘的庭院教堂、岩窟僧房与引水渠需经陡峭步道和台阶抵达，四周森林覆盖。修复期间开放曾受影响，但其所处环境仍是土耳其最壮观的之一。

Rize/Artvin, Türkiye · 土耳其里泽/阿尔特温

**UNESCO World Heritage**

No · 否

**Intro**

The Kaçkar Mountains form a dramatic range along the eastern Black Sea, with glaciated peaks, alpine lakes and traditional yayla settlements. Karagöl Sahara is a high-altitude lake in the forests of Artvin, near the Georgian border, surrounded by fir trees and steep mountains.
